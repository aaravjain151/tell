Reference voices

    Add a voice

    Record it now: press Record and read this aloud in your normal voice and pace (10 to 25 s), then Stop. It is transcribed on this machine; fix any wrong words below before adding.

    "Hey, it's me. I'm just reading this out loud so the app can learn how I sound. Let's meet on Tuesday afternoon, and I'll bring the notes. If that doesn't work, Wednesday morning is fine too."

    The clip stays on this machine in voices/. Do not use this to imitate someone to people who do not know, to deceive, or to commit fraud.

    Import from a longer recording

    On the command line, the cleanest 5 to 15 s is picked for you. The speaker and what they agreed to are required and never defaulted:

    python tools/import_voice.py --src rec.wav --transcript rec.txt \
  --name NAME --speaker "Full Name" \
  --attest "what they agreed to"
