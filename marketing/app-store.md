# App Store listing (draft — v2, once real calls work)

**Name:** Doublespeak — AI caller check  
**Subtitle:** Is that voice human, a bot, or a scam?  
**Promo text:** Every voice has a tell. Doublespeak checks unknown callers for synthetic voices and scam patterns — on your phone, never in a cloud.

**Description:**
Doublespeak listens with you and shows you what it hears: breath, pauses, pitch, script, and intent. In seconds you get one of four verdicts — Human, AI (identified itself), AI (undisclosed), or Scam pattern — and the exact reasons. Nothing you hear leaves your device; every verdict comes with a receipt you can verify.

- Works on calls you choose to check, voicemails, and clips.
- Shows the tells, never just a score.
- Flags AI callers that don't identify themselves (legit ones are required to).
- Family mode: share an allow-list and a safe word.

**Keywords:** scam call, AI voice, deepfake, voice clone, caller id, robocall, grandparent scam, privacy  
**What's new:** first release.

Rules: no "100%", no "blocks all scams", no comparisons by name in the listing.
