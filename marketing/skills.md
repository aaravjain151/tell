## Positioning
**Doublespeak: every voice has a tell.** The only AI-caller detector where the call never leaves your phone — and it shows you why.

## Pillars
1. **Proof, not promises** — the bytes-out meter, the signed receipt, the open eval.
2. **The tells** — short explainers on one tell each (no breath, metronomic pauses, "I completely understand").
3. **Family protection** — grandparent-scam stories told by the adult kids who caught them (with consent).
4. **The AI-caller etiquette** — legit bots say so; we celebrate the ones that do.

## Rules
- No unprovable claims ("catches 99%", "unbeatable"). Quote the eval numbers with the fixture caveat.
- No fake testimonials, no AI-generated "victims", no stock-photo grandmas.
- One CTA per post. Never "AI = scam"; always "AI × intent".
- Public stats must link to the primary source (FBI IC3, FTC, Hiya report).
