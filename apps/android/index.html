      Before you listen

      Doublespeak listens to this call through your microphone on your phone and sends the audio only to your own computer over the USB cable. Nothing is recorded or stored. Tell the other person if your state requires it.

      This works in speaker mode only: the call has to be on speakerphone.

      How speaker mode works

      Android does not give apps the audio of a phone call. So Doublespeak listens the way you do: through the microphone, while the call plays out loud on speakerphone.

      • It hears the caller from the speaker, your own voice, and the room.
      • It does not hear a call held to your ear, on Bluetooth, or on headphones.
      • Some phones silence the microphone for other apps during a call. If the level bars stay flat, this phone is doing that and Doublespeak cannot help on this call.
      • Accuracy is lower than with clean audio, so the engine caps how sure it says it is.
      • Keep this screen on. If you switch apps, Android may stop the microphone.
