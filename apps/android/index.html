      Before you listen

      Tell listens to this call through your microphone on your phone and sends the audio only to your own computer over the USB cable. Nothing is recorded or stored. Tell the other person if your state requires it.

      This works in speaker mode only: the call has to be on speakerphone.

      Listen when you answer

      When you answer a call, Tell listens through your microphone in speaker mode and sends the audio only to your own computer over the USB cable. Nothing is recorded or stored. Tell the other person if your state requires it.

      • Android asks for the Phone permission next. Tell uses it only to see that a call was answered and when it ended. It never reads numbers, contacts or call history.
      • Put the call on speaker. Android gives apps no call audio; Tell hears the speaker through the microphone.
      • A notification shows while Tell listens, with a Stop button. When Tell is not on screen, Android 11 and newer ask you to tap Listen first.
      • Keep the USB cable plugged in. If Tell cannot reach your computer, it says so in a notification.

      How speaker mode works

      Android does not give apps the audio of a phone call. So Tell listens the way you do: through the microphone, while the call plays out loud on speakerphone.

      • It hears the caller from the speaker, your own voice, and the room.
      • It does not hear a call held to your ear, on Bluetooth, or on headphones.
      • Some phones silence the microphone for other apps during a call. If the level bars stay flat, this phone is doing that and Tell cannot help on this call.
      • Accuracy is lower than with clean audio, so the engine caps how sure it says it is.
      • Keep this screen on. If you switch apps, Android may stop the microphone.
