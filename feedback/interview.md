# Interview script (15 min, past-behavior based)

1. Tell me about the last call you answered from a number you didn't know. What happened?
2. When did you last suspect a caller wasn't who they said? What tipped you off?
3. Has anyone in your family received a call pretending to be a relative in trouble? Walk me through it.
4. What do you do today when a call feels off? (hang up, google, call someone?)
5. Do you use any call-screening app? What made you install it — and what made you stop, if you did?
6. If a tool said "this caller is probably synthetic", what would you want to see next to that?
7. (Show the verdict card.) What do you think happened here? What would you do now?
8. What would make you distrust this verdict?
9. Would you be comfortable if the app analysed the call on your phone? What if it sent the audio to a company's servers?
10. Who in your family would you set this up for? What would stop you?
11. If you had to pay for it, what would feel fair — and what would feel wrong?

Never ask "would you use this?" cold. Note verbatim quotes.
