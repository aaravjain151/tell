# feedback/ — skills.md

## Purpose
Learn whether anyone would use it (the track's last judging question) and where it's wrong on real people.

## Files
- `interview.md` — 11 past-behavior questions, no leading.
- `recruiting.md` — 10 people from the wedge (adult children of 60+ parents) in two weeks.
- `feedback-log.md` — one line per observation with tags.

## Weekly loop
Mon: pick 3 observations → Tue: decide (pm.py decide) → Wed–Fri: ship → next Mon: did the metric move?

## Metrics
| Metric | Now | Target |
|---|---|---|
| False positive on a real human clip (fixture) | 0/24 | < 5% on real clips |
| "Would you install this for a parent?" (yes/10) | – | ≥ 6 |
| Time to understand the verdict card (s) | – | < 10 |

## Rules
- Record interviews only with consent; store notes, never audio.
- Tags: `#fp` false positive, `#fn` missed, `#copy` wording, `#trust`, `#install`.
